Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

We want you to feel confident before you pay. This policy explains when a refund is and isn't possible.

Before work starts

If you've paid but work has not yet started (status is "PAID" and not yet "WORK STARTED"), you can request a full refund by contacting us within 48 hours of payment.

After work starts

Once a project has moved to "Work Started", setup and strategy work is already underway. Refunds after this point are considered case by case, and any completed work (e.g. a live ad campaign already launched, a website section already built) is not refundable.

Monthly services

Monthly services (Social Media Marketing, SEO) are billed one month at a time. Cancelling stops future billing; the current month already paid for is not refunded once work for that month has begun.

Ad spend

Any budget paid directly to Meta or Google for advertising is governed by their own refund policies, not ours — our refund policy only covers our service fee.

How to request a refund

Email teamonlygrowth@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp with your order details. Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method via Razorpay, typically within 5–7 business days.

Non-refundable items

Completed deliverables (e.g. delivered creatives, a launched campaign, a completed website) are not refundable once delivered.
