Process

From first message to a completed project.

The same clear path for every service — fixed-price or quote-based.

  1. 1

    Pick a service

    Browse services, open the one you need, and read the full detail page — pricing, inclusions and timeline up front.

  2. 2

    Get Started or Get Quote

    Fixed-price services go straight to a requirement form. Variable-scope services (like Website Development) start with a quick scoping conversation.

  3. 3

    Requirement form + account

    Tell us what you need, then create your TeamOnlyGrowth account so everything is saved to your dashboard.

  4. 4

    Requirement confirmation

    We review what you've submitted and confirm it's clear enough to start — or ask a follow-up question first.

  5. 5

    Quote (if needed)

    For starting-price services, you'll receive a firm quote here. Nothing is charged until you approve it.

  6. 6

    Payment

    Secure payment via Razorpay. We verify every payment on our server before marking an order as paid — your payment details never touch our database.

  7. 7

    Work starts

    Your project status moves to “Work Started” in your dashboard, and stays updated as we go.

  8. 8

    Delivery / completion

    You get the agreed deliverables, and the project is marked “Completed” in your dashboard.
