Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

What we collect

When you use the web assistant, your messages are sent to our server function and then to an AI provider (currently Groq) to generate a reply. Contact forms collect name, email, phone (optional), and message via Netlify Forms.

Voice

Microphone audio is processed in your browser for speech recognition when you use voice features. We do not operate a separate audio storage system on this site unless a future feature is explicitly added and disclosed.

Cookies / local storage

The site may use browser storage for UI preferences. We do not sell personal data.

Providers

Hosting: Netlify. AI inference: Groq (or configured providers). Form storage: Netlify Forms.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact form or WhatsApp listed on the site.
