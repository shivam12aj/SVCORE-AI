Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

TeamOnlyGrowth ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's protected when you use this website or work with us.

Information we collect

When you sign up, submit an enquiry, request a quote, or make a payment, we collect: your name, email, WhatsApp number, city, business details, the service you're interested in, and any optional details you choose to share (website, Instagram, budget).

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, deliver services you've purchased, send order/project updates, and process payments. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never store your card, UPI or bank details — Razorpay handles that directly, and we only receive confirmation of payment status.

Passwords

Your password is never stored in plain text. Authentication is handled by Supabase, which stores only a securely hashed version.

Data storage

Your account and project data is stored in our Supabase database with access controls (Row Level Security) that restrict it to you and authorized TeamOnlyGrowth admins only.

Third-party services

Depending on which integrations are active, limited data may also be processed by: Razorpay (payments), Google Sheets (internal reporting), Telegram (internal admin alerts), and an email delivery provider (transactional emails). None of these receive your password.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing teamonlygrowth@gmail.com or messaging us on WhatsApp.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email teamonlygrowth@gmail.com.
